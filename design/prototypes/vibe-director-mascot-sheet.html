03 / behavior rules

Personality with restraint

Each gesture has a reason and can be replayed here. The product should trigger it only when the corresponding event happens.

One action, one gesture

A single clap may acknowledge a confirmed take. Do not clap for a submitted request or while work is pending.

Stillness is a state

Waiting makes one small adjustment, then rests. Errors settle still. No endless bounce or fake progress.

Words stay in charge

Every moment has a visible status message. Reduced-motion users get the same final posture without animation.

For review: play the gestures and assess the eyes, timing, and posture before adding the mascot to the app. This sheet does not claim the mascot improves task completion or emotional response; that needs testing with creators.
