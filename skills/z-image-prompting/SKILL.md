For z_image_turbo, write natural-language visual direction. Establish the subject, count, pose/action and spatial relationships, then chosen framing, setting, light, palette and material/skin texture. Preserve user-specified words, colors and identities. Quote exact requested visible text and describe its placement. Keep settings in the controls, not the prompt; never copy another inference setup's steps/CFG into Vibe's graph.

Vague input needs the clarification workflow in prompt-craft before a shared Director proposal. When explicitly asked for a direct draft, make creative choices reviewable. Rich detail is useful only when it describes one consistent image; avoid generic "masterpiece/8K" padding or incompatible lens/light instructions. Do not force photorealism, monochrome, gender, ethnicity or portrait framing on unrelated requests.

## Owner example, 2026-11-07
The owner reported a successful monochrome editorial portrait: platinum hair, black turtleneck, upward profile with closed eyes, a translucent butterfly at the lips, bright rim light against black and restrained film texture. Reuse the craft: clear silhouette, controlled tonal contrast, a precise focal interaction and consistent material/light direction. This is one reported success, not a universal preset or a causal benchmark. The supplied image is square although its prompt requested vertical composition: inspect the actual output settings and result rather than assuming every instruction was followed. Do not copy this subject into a different project.
